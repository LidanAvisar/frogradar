Acme Registry pricing

Updated October 17, 2026

Acme Registry is a universal artifact repository for every package format your teams build and consume, with security scanning built in.

Free

$0 per user/month

  • Unlimited public repositories for open source projects
  • 2 GB of artifact storage included for every workspace
  • Community support through the public forum

Team

$4 per user/month

  • Everything in Free, plus private repositories for teams
  • 10 GB of artifact storage included for every workspace
  • Vulnerability scanning for container images and npm packages
  • Email support with a two business day response time

Enterprise

$21 per user/month

  • Everything in Team, plus single sign-on and audit logs
  • 100 GB of artifact storage included for every workspace
  • Malicious package blocking for npm, PyPI and Maven
  • Dedicated support with a four hour response time

Frequently asked questions

How is storage counted? Storage is the total size of all artifacts kept in your repositories at the end of each day.

Can I change plans later? Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time and the change applies to the next billing cycle.
